Created for Chuck Cooper. This personalized autonomous-workflow package supports participating in and following motocross.

Autonomous modules

  • Track Finder — discover relevant tracks and riding opportunities.
  • Event Monitor — organize race and practice schedules.
  • Weather + Track Conditions — check forecasts and flag conditions that may affect riding.
  • Bike Maintenance Manager — track service intervals, oil, filters, chain, tires, suspension and bike hours.
  • Ride / Performance Log — preserve conditions, setup notes and observations.
  • Motocross Community Scout — organize relevant motocross activity and opportunities.
  • Gear / Parts Assistant — research compatible gear and replacement parts.
  • Highlight Creator — structure ride-day photos/video into recap and social-content plans.
  • Race-Day Mode — assemble weather, departure timing, bike prep, fuel, gear, maintenance status, event information and rider notes into one briefing.

Race-Day Mode input

Provide riding location, date, bike details and any known event information. The workflow returns a consolidated preparation brief and checklist. Verify track rules, event schedules, weather and safety-critical information with authoritative sources before riding.
