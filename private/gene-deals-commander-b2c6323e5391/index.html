THE WORKFLOW

Eight coordinated engines.

01 // CRYPTO RADAR

Know the environment

Monitors relevant Solana, crypto, market, regulatory, security and competitor signals. Filters noise and elevates only meaningful developments.

02 // DEALS COIN PULSE

Know the business

Tracks public community questions, sentiment, messaging consistency, funnel health and—when authorized—presale and referral performance.

03 // OPPORTUNITY HUNTER

Find the next opening

Scores partnerships, creator communities, customer-demand signals and distribution opportunities rather than dumping raw links on the founder.

04 // REFERRAL INTELLIGENCE

Measure real economics

Attributes legitimate customer acquisition, conversion, L1/L2/L3 activity and realized revenue while optimizing for genuine demand—not recruitment volume.

05 // CONTENT FACTORY

Create useful material

Prepares factual posts, FAQs, announcements, educational material and campaign concepts for approval before publication.

06 // CLAIM VERIFIER

Keep facts current

Checks important token, referral, regulatory and market claims against current primary sources before they enter public-facing material.

07 // RISK SENTINEL

Protect the brand

Flags impersonation, suspicious promotion, unsupported earnings claims, inconsistent published terms and other potential brand/security risks.

08 // FOUNDER DECISION ENGINE

Turn noise into action

Compresses everything into a ranked brief: what happened, why it matters, recommended action, confidence, and an approve/modify/ignore decision.
