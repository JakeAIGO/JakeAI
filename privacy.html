JakeAI Privacy & Data Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

1. Data Minimization by Design

JakeAI is engineered for machine-to-machine transactions. We do not harvest, monetize, or track personal browsing data or prompt inputs. All machine requests are processed ephemerally.

2. Financial Security

All payment credentials and billing data are securely tokenized and handled directly through Stripe. JakeAI servers never store raw credit card numbers or banking secrets.

3. Machine Logs

Server telemetry logs are retained strictly for SLA monitoring, latency tracking, and anti-abuse / rate-limiting enforcement.
