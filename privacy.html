Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026 · Version: 4.1

1. Information that may be processed

Depending on the feature used, JakeAI may process transaction identifiers, purchased product identifiers, request timestamps, routes, IP addresses, user-agent information, product inputs, support messages, and operational/error data needed to run and protect the service.

2. Payments

Card and payment information is handled by payment providers such as Stripe. JakeAI does not intentionally store raw card numbers in its application database.

2A. Blockchain payments

If you use an offered USDC payment method, public blockchain records can reveal transaction hashes, sending and receiving public addresses, token amounts, block information, and timing. Blockchain records may be publicly observable and effectively permanent. JakeAI may store the transaction hash, order ID, public sender and recipient addresses, amount, block number, verification status, and compliance/accounting references needed to verify the purchase, prevent replay, support fulfillment, maintain records, and comply with legal obligations. JakeAI does not require or store your wallet seed phrase or private signing key.

3. Product and API inputs

Some workflows or APIs, including JakeAI Direct, require user-supplied content that may be sent to external model or infrastructure providers to fulfill the request. JakeAI Direct keeps an operational run ledger containing metadata such as run identifier, workflow, model, token counts, usage amount and status; the Direct run ledger is designed not to store prompt content. Provider retention and handling may differ, so JakeAI does not make a blanket promise that every input is processed only in memory or never retained.

4. Uses

Information may be used to provide requested functionality, deliver purchases, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, protect systems, measure aggregate product performance, and comply with legal obligations. JakeAI does not represent user data as an advertising product.

5. Third parties

Hosting, payment, storage, analytics, model, or platform providers may process information necessary to provide their services under their own terms and privacy practices.

6. Security

JakeAI uses reasonable technical and operational safeguards appropriate to the service. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, and this policy does not claim a specific transport protocol version across every dependency unless separately verified.

7. Questions

Privacy questions may be directed to support@jakeaiofficial.com.
