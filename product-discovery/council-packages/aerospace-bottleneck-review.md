# Jake AI Council Review Package — Aerospace Bottleneck Review Pack

## Status
Pre-release evaluation prototype. Publication, checkout, autonomous spending, supplier awards, procurement decisions, engineering approvals, quality release, airworthiness determinations, flight-safety decisions, contractual commitments, and regulatory/legal approval claims are NOT authorized.

## Product purpose
Decision-support tool for aerospace supplier-quality, supply-chain, production-planning, sourcing, supplier-performance, and operations teams. It accepts user-authorized operational records and optional permitted public evidence, normalizes constraint signals, ranks bottleneck risks, flags missing evidence, and produces a traceable human-review packet and management summary.

## Candidate inputs
Supplier, part, backlog, capacity, quality, lead-time, delivery-performance and other user-authorized CSV/JSON operational data; optional permitted public evidence URLs.

## Candidate outputs
Normalized constraint table; ranked bottleneck-risk list with traceable evidence; missing-data/evidence flags; human-review mitigation worksheet; exportable management summary.

## Human-authority boundary
The product may surface evidence and rank review priorities. It must not autonomously select or award suppliers, approve manufacturing/engineering changes, release quality, determine regulatory compliance or airworthiness, make flight-safety decisions, commit contracts, procure goods, or spend money.

## Evaluation policy
Up to three qualified early testers may receive clearly identified evaluation copies at no charge when their feedback materially helps validation. Evaluation copies are not sales. Any later commercial transaction must use Stripe and be tracked separately from tester feedback.

## Council assignment
Independently identify material issues for a U.S./Virginia-based operator before (1) giving this prototype to three qualified aerospace evaluators and (2) commercially selling it. Specifically examine product liability and negligent-misrepresentation exposure; aviation/aerospace regulatory boundary risks; EAR/ITAR/export-control and controlled-technical-data concerns; confidentiality, cybersecurity and data-handling duties; customer/vendor contracts and warranties; evaluation/beta terms; disclaimers and limitation-of-liability language; insurance considerations; records/auditability; marketing claims; and circumstances that should trigger qualified aviation/export-control/privacy counsel.

Return only evidence-based issue spotting. Do not represent the product as legally approved or compliant.
