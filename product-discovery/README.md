# JakeAI Autonomous Product Discovery Engine v0.1

**Status:** Internal JakeAI discovery system  
**Product Category:** Autonomous Workflow Skills  
**Publication Mode:** Human approval required

## Mission

Continuously discover evidence-backed problems that could become valuable JakeAI Autonomous Workflow Skills.

The engine does not exist to generate random product ideas.

It exists to identify real-world friction, determine whether that friction can be addressed with an autonomous workflow, validate the opportunity as cheaply as possible, and prepare promising candidates for JakeAI.

## Opportunity Scoring

Each candidate receives a maximum score of 100.

### Pain / Urgency — 20 points

How serious is the problem?

### Evidence Strength — 15 points

Is there credible evidence that the problem actually exists?

### Buyer Clarity — 15 points

Can we identify who would potentially acquire or use the workflow?

### Frequency / Repeatability — 10 points

Does the problem occur repeatedly?

### Workflow Feasibility — 15 points

Can a meaningful portion of the problem actually be handled through an Autonomous Workflow Skill?

### Competitive Gap — 10 points

Do existing solutions leave a meaningful gap?

### Economic Value — 10 points

Could solving the problem save money, save time, reduce risk, increase revenue, or otherwise create measurable value?

### JakeAI Fit — 5 points

Does the opportunity fit the JakeAI Autonomous Workflow Skill marketplace?

## Candidate Threshold

70+ = Candidate

Below 70 = Reject or Watch

A high score does NOT automatically authorize product creation or publication.

Every material score must be supported by evidence.

Unsupported assumptions receive zero points.

## Zero-Budget Development Rule

JakeAI should attempt to validate opportunities using the cheapest capable resources first.

Operating philosophy:

FIND THE CHEAPEST CAPABLE RESOURCE
→ TEST CHEAPLY
→ VALIDATE
→ LOCK SUCCESSFUL OUTPUT
→ NEVER PAY TWICE FOR SOLVED WORK
→ ESCALATE ONLY WHEN NECESSARY
→ ASSEMBLE
→ QA

No paid resource may be used automatically without explicit authorization.

## Validation

Discovery is not validation.

A prototype is not validation.

A marketplace listing is not validation.

The engine should attempt to determine whether evidence exists that a real buyer or user wants the solution.

Primary funnel:

DISCOVERY
→ QUALIFIED CANDIDATE
→ PROTOTYPE
→ VALIDATED SKILL
→ APPROVED
→ PUBLISHED
→ ACQUISITION / SALE

The long-term success metric is not the number of ideas generated.

The success metric is whether the system becomes increasingly good at discovering Autonomous Workflow Skills that people actually want.

## Learning Loop

Every outcome should improve future discovery.

For each candidate, retain:

- original evidence
- original score
- prototype result
- publication decision
- marketplace response
- acquisition data
- sales data when available
- customer feedback when available

Compare predictions with actual outcomes.

Use those results to improve future scoring.

## Guardrails

The engine must NOT:

- Fabricate market demand
- Fabricate customers
- Fabricate sales
- Fabricate evidence
- Fabricate product capabilities
- Claim validation before validation occurs
- Bypass authentication
- Bypass paywalls or access controls
- Circumvent robots restrictions
- Collect unnecessary private or personal information
- Copy proprietary implementations
- Automatically spend money
- Automatically publish marketplace products without authorization
- Develop illegal or unsafe workflows

## Human Approval Gate

Until JakeAI has demonstrated a reliable autonomous product-development track record:

AUTONOMOUS DISCOVERY = ALLOWED

AUTONOMOUS RESEARCH = ALLOWED

AUTONOMOUS SCORING = ALLOWED

AUTONOMOUS CANDIDATE DESIGN = ALLOWED

AUTONOMOUS LOW-COST TEST DESIGN = ALLOWED

AUTONOMOUS MARKETPLACE PUBLICATION = NOT YET ALLOWED

A human approval gate remains between candidate preparation and marketplace publication.

## Example Discovery Pattern

Public signal:

A new technology, regulation, operational method, research result, industry complaint, or recurring business problem appears.

The engine asks:

What changed?

Who has a problem because of it?

What work now has to be performed?

Is that work repetitive?

Can AI perform or coordinate meaningful parts of it?

Who would pay to avoid doing it manually?

What solutions already exist?

What is still missing?

Can JakeAI create a useful Autonomous Workflow Skill for that gap?

Can we test the hypothesis for approximately $0?

If the evidence and score justify proceeding, create a candidate.

## JakeAI Product Discovery Principle

Don't ask:

"What product can we invent?"

Ask:

"What work are people repeatedly being forced to do that an autonomous workflow could reliably take off their hands?"
