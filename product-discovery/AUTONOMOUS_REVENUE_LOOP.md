# Jake AI Autonomous Revenue Loop

## Objective
Prove one closed-loop transaction from independently observed demand to a paid customer outcome.

## Operating loop
1. Discover public demand signals with evidence URLs and timestamps.
2. Cluster repeated pain points; reject weak/single-source ideas.
3. Score candidates on urgency, buyer clarity, buildability, differentiation, distribution reach, support burden, and legal/safety risk.
4. Select the highest-scoring low-risk candidate.
5. Produce a minimum useful product with reproducible tests and evidence.
6. Run QA, security, and legal/liability gates. Fail closed.
7. Set price and packaging from evidence; no unsupported claims.
8. Require explicit publication authorization before production listing/checkout is enabled.
9. Create a NEW JKI social story asset showing the specific change since the previous update; JKI canonical logo/avatar remains consistent while scene/action changes.
10. Distribute through approved connected channels; verify sent status and log service URLs.
11. Monitor visits, checkout starts, purchases, refunds, failures, and customer feedback.
12. Feed evidence back into scoring and iterate or retire.

## Success condition
At least one unrelated customer pays for a factory-discovered product and receives the promised deliverable successfully.

## Guardrails
- No autonomous spending.
- No fabricated demand, validation, live-data, legal-compliance, or model-participation claims.
- No production publication or checkout without the repository's required authorization gates.
- Safety-critical/regulatory candidates require documented heightened review.
- Metering, entitlement, fulfillment, and delivery fail closed.
- Record evidence sufficient to reproduce why a candidate was selected and why each gate passed.

## Current milestone
Build the scheduler/event-driven runner that can execute discovery and scoring without a human saying “Go,” while keeping build/publication/spending gates conservative.
