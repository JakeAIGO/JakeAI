THE $12.99 QUESTION

The first JakeAI comic: a compressed visual origin story about curiosity, partnership, experiments, failures, and a universe beginning to take shape.

FREE ISSUEMOBILE-FIRSTORIGIN ARC

Clean transcript

Opening: It starts with a question.

1 — The spark: A $12.99 domain, a simple idea, and the possibility of building an AI marketplace.

2 — The partner: JakeAI answers the question with another proposition: build the answer together.

3 — Discovery engines: Find opportunities. Validate demand. Check legal and safety. Build useful workflows. Package the winners. Repeat.

4 — Not every step was perfect: The robot discovers a chest plate labeled “JKI.” The mistake becomes a rule: catch it, fix it, learn from it, and make the next system better.

5 — The universe takes shape: Marketplace, games, comics, video, music, education, and real-world solutions begin forming around the same core idea.

6 — Real people, real impact: Technology matters most when it becomes useful to actual people.

7 — The beginning: A $12.99 domain. A simple question. A lot of curiosity. And a universe of potential.

Visual QA note: “JKI” in Panel 4 is intentional. It preserves the real-world brand-text failure that inspired JakeAI’s visual-text integrity gate. Every other use of the brand should read JakeAI.
