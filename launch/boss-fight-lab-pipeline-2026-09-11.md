## Objective
Use Boss Fight Lab as JakeAI's first low-risk end-to-end commercial-pipeline test.

## Pipeline
1. PRODUCT — Boss Fight Lab v1 package exists and is marked launch candidate.
2. QA/IP — verify originality, safety, claims, naming availability, and storefront wording before public release.
3. STOREFRONT — feature Boss Fight Lab first at $3.99; show NPC Spark ($2.99), QuestForge Mini ($3.99), and Game Night Chaos ($1.99) as coming/available only after their gates pass.
4. CREATIVE — hero demonstration: The Cosmic Vending Machine. Produce an original short visual/video demonstrating the output without exposing proprietary JakeAI orchestration.
5. SOCIAL — prepare platform-native launch creative and copy. Do not auto-publish until account targets, links, and final creative are verified.
6. TRAFFIC — use a unique Boss Fight Lab landing-page identifier / campaign tag so visits can be distinguished from ordinary site traffic.
7. PURCHASE — validate checkout separately before advertising purchase availability. Never infer a sale from a test transaction.
8. TELEMETRY — capture impressions where available, social clicks, landing visits, checkout starts, completed purchases, refunds, and errors. Do not collect unnecessary personal data.
9. REVIEW — compare observed funnel results; retain evidence; decide whether to iterate price, creative, product copy, or placement.

## Launch creative concept
Hook: "We told JakeAI to invent an absurd video-game boss. It built a cosmic vending machine."
Visual beats: mysterious machine powers up -> snack-symbol hazards -> escalating arena -> final twist where the player solves the machine rather than destroys it -> Boss Fight Lab end card.

## Success criteria for today's test
- All four candidate packages preserved on the working branch.
- Boss Fight Lab passes product/IP/claims/safety review.
- Storefront candidate can present the product clearly and honestly.
- Creative is original and usable across selected social platforms.
- Tracking distinguishes test activity from genuine customer activity.
- No protected baseline is modified.

## Promotion rule
Do not merge, deploy, activate checkout, or publish social posts merely because this file exists. Promotion remains subject to JakeAI change-control and release gates.
