JakeAI Tattoo is a concept-development and creative-workflow product. A qualified tattoo professional remains responsible for final placement, stencil preparation, line weight, shading, pigments, skin assessment, application, and aftercare. Names, dates, coordinates, Roman numerals, foreign-language text, and other permanent text should be verified before final export.

AI can help untangle the brief. It still cannot hold a tattoo machine responsibly.

Marketplace sellers must have the rights necessary to list and license submitted material. Reference images are context, not permission to duplicate another creator's work. Project lineage can preserve human creative decisions and revisions, but it is not a guarantee of copyright protection. JakeAI does not auto-post creator campaigns externally without approval.
