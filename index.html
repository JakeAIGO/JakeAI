The Series
Core Concept

The Unseen Physical Engine

Real people. Real machines. Real stakes.

The future is not being drafted in slide decks or software applications. It is being physically built in factories, high-voltage substations, drilling sites, foundries, heavy construction corridors, and advanced industrial facilities most people will never see.

Modern civilization is meeting a physical wall: artificial intelligence supercomputing clusters require multi-gigawatt continuous power, heavy industry is transitioning off fossil fuels, and North America’s electrical grid is operating near its capacity limit.

GRIDWORKS strips away artificial manufactured drama to document the actual pressure cooker of industrial reality: critical path deadlines with millions at stake, high-voltage tie-ins under active load, and the relentless craftsmanship required to keep the modern world operating.

The Physical Challenge
Gigawatt-Scale Load Demands
Hyperscale computing and electrification are straining substations and transmission lines faster than traditional utilities can build.
The Supply Chain Reality
Critical Infrastructure Backlogs
Extra-high-voltage transformers face 3- to 4-year lead times. A single failure impacts entire regional grids.
The Show Promise
Industrial Scale Documentary
Kinetic, high-mobility filmmaking taking viewers behind perimeter gates into live, active facilities.

Jason Gets Us Inside

Jason Horn is not an academic theoretician, nor does he pretend to be the world's universal authority. He is an active contractor and commercial energy broker who lives and works in the industrial space. He speaks the language of high-voltage linemen, project superintendents, crane riggers, and utility executives.

In GRIDWORKS, the engineers, fabricators, field scientists, and heavy machine operators are the true experts. Jason acts as the curious, grounded surrogate for the audience: earning trust on the jobsite, getting cameras behind restricted perimeters, and asking the practical, mechanical questions viewers want answered.

“The engineers and operators keeping this infrastructure running are dealing with immense physical forces every day. My job isn't to lecture—it's to get our cameras through the front gate, step onto the floor with them, and see how the work actually gets done.”
— Jason Horn, Creator & Host
Format Architecture

The GRIDWORKS Engine

A repeatable 7-beat narrative structure proving series longevity across seasons.

BEAT 01
The Problem
A concrete physical bottleneck or infrastructural vulnerability threatening modern operations.
BEAT 02
The People
The frontline specialists, master riggers, lead metallurgists, and facility operators tasked with solving it.
BEAT 03
Get Inside
Jason navigates the high-security perimeter, safety clearances, and badging to embed directly with the crew.
BEAT 04
The Machine
The massive, awe-inspiring hardware deployed: high-pressure turbines, 100k-RPM impellers, or 500-ton cranes.
BEAT 05
The Pressure
The operational crisis point: live high-voltage tie-ins, 1,000,000-volt dielectric tests, or strict outage windows.
BEAT 06
The Solution
The technical execution: precision installation, pressure stabilization, or successful trial ignition under live load.
BEAT 07
What Changes?
The tangible ripple effect on the regional grid, national resilience, and the roadmap of what comes next.

Development Slate

Proof-of-Franchise Episodes

Five anchor concepts demonstrating range, scale, and access across the slate.

Episode 01 World: Power

AI / Data-Center Power

When hyperscale artificial intelligence computing clusters require multi-gigawatt continuous loads, 5-year utility interconnection queues threaten to halt technological expansion.

The Operational Challenge

Islanding an autonomous 350 MW data center campus using high-efficiency modular engines and massive liquid-cooled battery banks.

On-Camera Access

Jason embeds inside the server halls and engine vaults as operators manage microsecond frequency spikes when massive training runs fire up.

Episode 02 World: Build

Transformer Manufacturing

The hidden chokepoint of the continent’s electrical infrastructure: 200-ton Extra High Voltage (EHV) step-up transformers facing multi-year delivery lead times.

The Operational Challenge

Precision fabrication, core stacking, multi-day vacuum dry-out cycles, and high-stakes factory testing of a 500kV substation transformer.

On-Camera Access

Jason steps into the fabrication bay with master coil winders and witnesses a 1,000,000-volt impulse dielectric discharge test.

Episode 03 World: Power

Deep Geothermal

Tapping the boundless heat beneath the Earth's crust without being constrained to rare volcanic regions or porous hot aquifers.

The Operational Challenge

Injecting fluids into hot, dry crystalline rock under 10,000+ PSI to turn subterranean geology into a high-pressure 24/7 hydraulic battery.

On-Camera Access

Jason embeds on active drilling pads and high-pressure manifold decks, tracking subterranean telemetry and supercritical surface turbines.

Episode 04 World: Materials

Electric Steel & Advanced Manufacturing

Decarbonizing heavy industrial foundation: replacing centuries of metallurgical coking coal with high-amperage direct electrical electrolysis.

The Operational Challenge

Smelting raw iron ore into liquid steel at 1,600°C using clean electricity, exhausting pure breathable oxygen as the sole chemical byproduct.

On-Camera Access

Jason dons high-temperature protective gear alongside foundry operators as white-hot molten iron flows directly from active electrolytic cells.

Episode 05 World: Frontier

Advanced & Portable Nuclear

Providing continuous, hardened, zero-carbon power for remote installations and defense networks resilient against catastrophic grid outages.

The Operational Challenge

Deploying containerized 1MW–5MW microreactors fueled by meltdown-proof TRISO particles, engineered for rapid transport and plug-and-play microgrids.

On-Camera Access

Jason examines precision containment systems, air-mobility transport skids, and unassisted passive-cooling safety mechanisms.

Execution Framework

Lean, Mobile Production Architecture

Maximum on-screen scale delivered through an agile 3-person strike crew.

Crew Unit 01

A-Camera DP (Handheld / Gimbal)

Crew Unit 02

B-Camera & Certified Drone Operator

Crew Unit 03

Dedicated Field Sound Mixer

Multi-channel wireless lavs shielded against high-RF and electromagnetic interference, paired with directional ambient industrial recording.
