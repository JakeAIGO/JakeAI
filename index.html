About JakeAI

Build in public. Keep the engine private.

JakeAI is an evolving ecosystem of autonomous workflow skills, creative tools, games, media, APIs, and experiments. The public site shows products, demonstrations, and outcomes—not proprietary prompts, orchestration logic, internal architecture, or protected production methods.

  • New products are developed as isolated candidates before promotion.
  • Checkout and delivery stay gated until the product is actually ready.
  • Existing products remain visible with truthful status labels.
  • Unrelated operating brands are not presented as part of JakeAI unless explicitly approved.
