CASTING REALITY CHECK

Polished, not fictionalized.

A casting headshot has to help someone recognize the person who actually walks into the room. The workflow is designed around that constraint.

What we preserve

  • ✓ recognizable facial structure and proportions
  • ✓ current apparent age and natural skin texture
  • ✓ hair, facial hair and eye characteristics
  • ✓ believable wardrobe, lighting and background

What we reject

  • ✕ face reshaping or “beauty filter” identity drift
  • ✕ unrealistic age reduction or body alteration
  • ✕ invented tattoos, scars, hair or defining features
  • ✕ images that look more like AI art than you
