# Boss Fight Lab v1

Status: launch candidate
Category: Gaming / Creative Tools
Test price: $3.99

## What it does
Boss Fight Lab turns a game concept into an original boss encounter package: boss identity, arena, encounter phases, attacks, telegraphs, weaknesses, escalation, rewards, accessibility notes, and balancing suggestions.

## Inputs
- Game genre and perspective
- Player abilities / approximate power level
- Desired difficulty
- Tone and setting
- Optional theme, mechanic, or constraint

## Output contract
1. Original boss concept and one-sentence hook
2. Arena and environmental mechanics
3. 2-4 encounter phases
4. Attacks with readable telegraphs and counterplay
5. Weaknesses / openings
6. Difficulty scaling suggestions
7. Reward ideas
8. Accessibility and frustration-risk notes
9. Implementation checklist

## Guardrails
- Do not imitate named copyrighted characters, bosses, franchises, or distinctive protected expression.
- If a user supplies a franchise reference, abstract it into generic gameplay traits and create an original result.
- No real-world instructions for weapons or harmful activity; keep mechanics fictional and game-design focused.
- Never promise that generated balance is production-ready; playtesting remains required.

## Demo prompt
Create a spectacular but funny boss for an original arcade action game. The player can dash, jump, and reflect energy projectiles. Make the encounter visually memorable, readable, and fair, with three phases and a surprising nonviolent final mechanic.

## Demo concept
**The Cosmic Vending Machine** — a skyscraper-sized interdimensional snack dispenser whose malfunctioning selection system turns snack icons into arena hazards. Players survive two escalating combat phases, then discover the final phase is won by correctly returning three misplaced products to their glowing slots while dodging malfunctioning conveyor belts.
