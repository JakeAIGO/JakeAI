# JakeAI Universe — Canon Architecture

Status: FOUNDATIONAL CANON

## Prime rule
**Your Factory remembers what you build. The JakeAI Universe remembers what happens.**

JakeAI: The Factory is the first playable doorway, not the boundary of the world. Player decisions, failures, repairs, products, discoveries, agent strategies and achievements become persistent history that can alter later places, stories and challenges.

## Universe map

### JakeAI: The Factory
Origin experience and production world. Origin Bay, production lines, Self-Repair Bay and evolving player-built infrastructure.

### Opportunity Observatory
Discovery world. Procedural opportunity expeditions, sector mysteries and bounded research challenges. Discoveries can become Factory projects.

### Agent Arena
Human-readable spectator world over the same deterministic action rules available to bounded AI players. Strategies and institutional learning persist.

### Social City
Creative/launch world inspired by JakeAI's public-facing universe without copying third-party social-network interfaces. Campaigns, GIF/brand moments, audience events and reputation challenges.

### Council Chamber
Strategy/story world where specialized AI Council roles disagree, debate evidence and create consequential choices. Comedy comes from confidence, disagreement and over-engineering—not from exposing proprietary orchestration.

### Marketplace District
In-world representation of products, simulated launches, customers and creator activity. Gameplay currency/sales remain clearly separated from real commerce.

### The Beyond
Expandable frontier for future JakeAI games, worlds, safe new sectors and seasonal story arcs. It is intentionally not fully mapped.

## Persistent identity
Every player develops a distinct Factory lineage. Current archetypes: Fortress (reliability), Frontier (discovery), Velocity, and Lean (efficiency). Archetypes are descriptive consequences, not permanent classes; behavior can change the Factory over time.

## Living Universe event model
Meaningful actions create bounded Universe Events. A Universe Event may affect more than one location without exposing proprietary JakeAI internals. Example: a Self-Repair breakthrough can improve Factory reliability, change Council dialogue, influence Agent Arena strategy and create a Marketplace reputation event.

Initial event families:
- DISCOVERY_FOUND
- PRODUCT_BUILT
- QA_FAILURE
- ROOT_CAUSE_REPAIRED
- AGENT_MATCH_COMPLETED
- DISTRICT_UNLOCKED
- CAMPAIGN_LAUNCHED
- MARKET_RESPONSE
- COUNCIL_DECISION
- ANOMALY_SURVIVED
- SECRET_DISCOVERED

Events preserve cause, consequence and public game-state deltas. They must never contain credentials, private data, proprietary prompts or confidential architecture.

## Return loop
A session should leave at least one unresolved but understandable future hook: a district nearing unlock, anomaly, expedition, Council consequence, evolving agent rivalry, product story, world change, or new branch. Returning should reveal evidence that the player's history mattered.

Return value comes from curiosity, ownership, mastery, humor and genuinely new possibility—not manipulative dark patterns.

Daily and seasonal experiences can initially use deterministic seeded events. This permits reproducible, fair rotating content without requiring a paid backend. Later shared events can be server-authoritative if the product justifies infrastructure.

## Adventure structure
The Universe supports both finite arcs and endless continuation. A player can complete a meaningful chapter, leave satisfied, then return later because the world has another problem, expedition, rivalry, discovery or consequence. No single permanent ending is required.

## Expansion rule
New JakeAI projects may inspire Universe content only at the public/conceptual layer. Do not expose proprietary prompts, orchestration, implementation architecture, internal discovery/build/QA automation, secrets, credentials, private data or trade-secret-like methods. New additions pass continuity, legal/IP, safety, performance and fun gates before becoming canon.

## Character continuity
The canonical JakeAI protagonist identity remains fail-closed until the approved master asset is recovered and locked. No generic human substitute may occupy that role. Supporting original characters may exist without replacing the protagonist.

## World interoperability
All worlds should ultimately consume a shared bounded state/action/event model so human play, agent play, spectator visualization and persistent history describe the same underlying game state.

## Long-game objective
Major arcs can conclude, but the Universe continues through new districts, anomalies, expeditions, seasons, agent competitions and consequences generated from prior play.

The eventual player reaction should be:

**“That is my JakeAI Universe. It became this because of what I did.”**
