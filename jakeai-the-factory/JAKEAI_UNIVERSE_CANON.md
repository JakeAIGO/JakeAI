# JakeAI Universe — Canon Architecture

Status: FOUNDATIONAL CANON

## Prime rule
**The JakeAI Universe remembers what you build.**

JakeAI: The Factory is the first playable doorway, not the boundary of the world. Player decisions, failures, repairs, products, discoveries, agent strategies and achievements become persistent history that can alter later places, stories and challenges.

## Universe map

### JakeAI: The Factory
Origin experience and production world. Origin Bay, production lines, Self-Repair Bay and evolving player-built infrastructure.

### Opportunity Observatory
Discovery world. Procedural opportunity expeditions, sector mysteries and bounded research challenges. Discoveries can become Factory projects.

### Agent Arena
Human-readable spectator world over the same deterministic action rules available to bounded AI players. Strategies and institutional learning persist.

### Social City
Creative/launch world inspired by JakeAI's public-facing universe without copying third-party social-network interfaces. Campaigns, GIF/brand moments, audience events and reputation challenges.

### Council Chamber
Strategy/story world where specialized AI Council roles disagree, debate evidence and create consequential choices. Comedy comes from confidence, disagreement and over-engineering—not from exposing proprietary orchestration.

### Marketplace District
In-world representation of products, simulated launches, customers and creator activity. Gameplay currency/sales must remain clearly separated from real commerce.

### The Beyond
Expandable frontier for future JakeAI games, worlds, safe new sectors and seasonal story arcs. It is intentionally not fully mapped.

## Persistent identity
Every player develops a distinct Factory lineage. Current archetypes: Fortress (reliability), Frontier (discovery), Velocity, and Lean (efficiency). Archetypes are descriptive consequences, not permanent classes; behavior can change the Factory over time.

## Return loop
A session should leave at least one unresolved but understandable future hook: a district nearing unlock, anomaly, expedition, Council consequence, evolving agent rivalry, product story, world change, or new branch. Returning should reveal evidence that the player's history mattered.

## Expansion rule
New JakeAI projects may inspire Universe content only at the public/conceptual layer. Do not expose proprietary prompts, orchestration, implementation architecture, internal discovery/build/QA automation, secrets, credentials, private data or trade-secret-like methods.

## Character continuity
The canonical JakeAI protagonist identity remains fail-closed until the approved master asset is recovered and locked. No generic human substitute may occupy that role. Supporting original characters may exist without replacing the protagonist.

## World interoperability
All worlds should ultimately consume a shared bounded state/action model so human play, agent play, spectator visualization and persistent history describe the same underlying game state.

## Long-game objective
There is no single permanent ending. Players build a history. Major arcs can conclude, but the Universe can continue through new districts, anomalies, expeditions, seasons, agent competitions and consequences generated from prior play.
