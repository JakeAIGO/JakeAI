Purchased and promotional credits stay separate.

Purchased credits, when the payment gate is enabled, are recorded separately from promotional credits. Redemptions use promotional credits first. The ledger is server-side and entitlements are permanent unless a transaction is reversed, refunded, fraudulent, or otherwise legally invalid.

Credits are redemption units—not a bank account, cryptocurrency, investment, or transferable currency.

Launch status: the credits ledger and My Library infrastructure are being made available before paid loading. JakeAI will not activate paid top-ups until the payment-platform compliance gate is cleared. The program is designed U.S.-only at launch, with no expiration or inactivity/service fees, and cash redemption/refunds only where applicable law requires it.

*“No cash withdrawal” is subject to non-waivable state or federal rights that may require cash redemption or refunds in specific circumstances.
