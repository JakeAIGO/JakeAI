Refund Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026 · Version: 3.0

1. Digital products

Because many JakeAI products are digital and may be delivered immediately after a verified payment, refunds are evaluated based on whether delivery occurred, whether the product materially matched its listing, and applicable law. Nothing in this policy limits non-waivable consumer rights.

2. Duplicate charges and failed delivery

If you believe you were charged more than once for the same purchase, or a verified payment did not result in the promised delivery, contact support@jakeaiofficial.com with the Stripe receipt or transaction identifier. JakeAI will review the transaction and, where appropriate or legally required, issue a refund or otherwise correct the failed delivery.

3. API and computational products

Metered or API products are not enabled for paid checkout unless their entitlement and fulfillment path has passed the applicable release gates. If such products are activated in the future, product-specific refund terms may apply.

4. Processing

Approved refunds are returned through the applicable payment processor to the original payment method where supported. Processing times depend on the payment provider and financial institution.
