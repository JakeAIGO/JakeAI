Refund Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026 · Version: 4.1

1. Digital products

Because many JakeAI products are digital and may be delivered immediately after a verified payment, refunds are evaluated based on whether delivery occurred, whether the product materially matched its listing, and applicable law. Nothing in this policy limits non-waivable consumer rights.

2. Duplicate charges and failed delivery

If you believe you were charged more than once for the same purchase, or a verified payment did not result in the promised delivery, contact support@jakeaiofficial.com with the Stripe receipt or transaction identifier. JakeAI will review the transaction and, where appropriate or legally required, issue a refund or otherwise correct the failed delivery.

3. Subscriptions and computational products

JakeAI Direct subscriptions may be canceled through the available billing-management flow, with access generally continuing through the paid period shown in the account unless applicable law or processor rules require otherwise. Included provider usage is an allowance, not an automatic overage authorization. Refund requests for subscription renewals or computational usage are reviewed based on delivery, access, material product mismatch, duplicate charges, technical failure, and applicable law.

4. Digital-asset payments

For an eligible USDC payment, contact support@jakeaiofficial.com with the JakeAI order ID and transaction hash if a verified payment was duplicated, misapplied, or did not produce the promised delivery. Approved digital-asset refunds may require address verification and may be returned through a supported method consistent with applicable law and operational controls. Transfers made on the wrong network, to the wrong address, or in an unsupported asset may be unrecoverable where JakeAI does not control the receiving address or asset.

5. Processing

Approved refunds are returned through the applicable payment processor to the original payment method where supported. Processing times depend on the payment provider and financial institution.
