Effective Date: September 7, 2026 | Version: 2.1

1. Digital Goods & Metered APIs

Due to the instant, irreversible computational fulfillment of digital downloads, real-time data feeds, and API executions, completed transactions are generally non-refundable once delivered.

If a network error, server fault (HTTP 500), or upstream timeout prevents delivery of a valid API response, or if an idempotency error causes an accidental duplicate charge, JakeAI's automated protocol will issue a full refund to the original payment method upon verification.

3. Support & Dispute Inquiries

For transaction disputes or questions regarding machine settlements, please contact our support desk at support@jakeaiofficial.com with your transaction ID or Stripe receipt.
