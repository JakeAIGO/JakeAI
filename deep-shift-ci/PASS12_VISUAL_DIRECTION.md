# Deep Shift — Pass 12 Visual Transformation

Visual target: atmospheric subterranean industrial sci-fi, readable at gameplay speed, clearly original, and materially beyond prototype boxes.

## Player silhouette
A compact tracked salvage-drill rig: tapered armored hull, bright canopy/visor, forward drill cone, side treads, rear energy core, directional work lights. The player must read instantly at 32–48 px gameplay scale.

## World
Layered rock strata rather than flat blocks; irregular tunnel edges; embedded ore veins/crystals; abandoned pipes, braces, cables and machinery silhouettes; dust motes and drilling debris; sector-specific ambient treatment.

## Sector identities
- Rust Veins — oxidized industrial earth, amber work lights, pipes and buried machinery.
- Crystal Fault — dark stone with luminous mineral formations and cool crystalline highlights.
- Magma Shelf — basalt, glowing fissures, heat haze/embers and dangerous lava readability.
- Ancient Works — buried machine complex, geometric ruins, cables, warning lights and gas vents.
- Core Nest — hostile deep cavern, dramatic red/orange core glow, organic/mineral corruption and boss arena framing.

## Creatures
No generic squares. Enemies need asymmetric silhouettes, eyes/cores, legs/mandibles or burrowing anatomy, hit reaction, and sector variation.

## Core Warden
Large multi-part subterranean machine-creature hybrid with a bright vulnerable core, armored ring/mandibles, telegraphed attacks and a silhouette substantially larger than ordinary enemies.

## Effects
Dig: rock chips + dust burst.
Ore: sparkle + pickup pulse.
Damage: flash + particles + brief screen emphasis.
Shock Pulse: expanding energy ring.
Seismic Charge: warning pulse then debris burst.
Extraction: beacon column / ring effect.
Boss: telegraphed hazard ring before damage.

## UI
Industrial instrument-panel look with clean hierarchy. Energy, ore requirement, credits, sector, abilities and extraction state must be readable without dominating the playfield.

## Gate
Do not call the build market-ready merely because it compiles. Visual promotion requires a human screenshot/play review confirming the scene no longer reads as primitive boxes/placeholders.
