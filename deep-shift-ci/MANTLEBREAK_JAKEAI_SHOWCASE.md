# MANTLEBREAK — Jake AI Showcase Game

Status: PROVISIONAL TITLE / JAKEAIOFFICIAL.COM FIRST-RELEASE STRATEGY
Previous working title: Deep Shift
Studio identity: Blazed Trail Studios (pending formal clearance)
Production system: Jake AI Autonomous Product Factory

## Product positioning
MANTLEBREAK is being developed first as a showcase product for JakeAIOfficial.com rather than assuming a Steam-first release. The game must demonstrate that an autonomous AI product factory can discover, design, engineer, test, repair, package, deploy, and iteratively improve a genuinely compelling playable product.

## Quality mandate
"AI made it" is not an excuse for mediocre quality. Publication is blocked until human playtesting confirms that the game is visually compelling, fun, responsive, understandable, and cool enough to represent Jake AI publicly.

## Core premise
Pilot a tracked industrial salvage-drill rig through increasingly hostile buried worlds. Dig, discover, fight, collect ore and artifacts, manage energy, deploy seismic and electrical tools, extract, upgrade, and descend deeper.

Progression: Rust Veins → Crystal Fault → Magma Shelf → Ancient Works → Core Nest.

Narrative direction: What begins as a salvage operation gradually reveals that the drilling is waking something buried below. The Core Warden is not merely a final boss; it is guarding something.
