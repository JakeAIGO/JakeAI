# Shiftforge Games

**Working studio identity for Deep Shift**

Tagline: **Built under pressure.**

Shiftforge Games is the game-development label for original commercial games produced through the Jake AI Autonomous Product Factory. Deep Shift is its first production experiment.

## Brand architecture

- Studio / developer: Shiftforge Games
- First title: Deep Shift
- Parent technology / autonomous production system: Jake AI Autonomous Product Factory
- Internal workflow product: Jake AI Autonomous Indie Game Studio Workflow

## Naming status

This is a working commercial name. A preliminary web collision screen found no obvious exact-match game studio in the searches performed on 2026-09-10. That is not a trademark clearance or legal opinion. Before commercial publication, run the legal/IP naming gate, including trademark and storefront collision checks.

## Brand direction

Industrial, engineered, subterranean, high-pressure, precise. The identity should be broad enough for future games beyond Deep Shift and should not imitate the visual identity of any existing game or studio.
