# JakeAI Connect QR creative standard

The permanent JakeAI Connect destination is **https://jakeaiofficial.com/connect/**.

## Default rule
Most JakeAI promotional social graphics should carry the reusable JakeAI Connect QR in a lower corner with the short label **SCAN TO CONNECT**. It is a brand utility, not the primary subject.

Use it by default for product, book, game, launch, event, announcement, YouTube end-card and similar promotional creative.

Skip it when the layout is intentionally minimal, when it would cover the primary subject or important copy, or when the format cannot preserve a reliably scannable code.

## Visual treatment
- Preferred placement: bottom-right; bottom-left when composition needs it.
- On a 1080px social canvas, target roughly 160–210px for the complete QR box and do not go below 150px.
- Keep a clean, high-contrast quiet zone around the code.
- A restrained JakeAI glow or frame is fine; never distort the QR itself.
- Do not place it over faces, key product details, legal copy, or visually noisy backgrounds.
- Use the canonical asset: `/assets/jakeai-connect-qr.svg`.
- Always scan-test the final exported graphic after resizing/compression.

## Homepage treatment
The homepage may expose Connect quietly: a small utility link plus a desktop QR badge. It should remain available without competing with the Foundry, Commission Bay, Arcade, Marketplace or problem-entry actions.

## Destination stability
Do not encode social-network-specific destinations in the standard QR. The permanent QR always resolves to the JakeAI-owned Connect hub so the downstream destinations can change without replacing published or printed QR assets.
