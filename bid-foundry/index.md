# JakeAI Bid Foundry RC4
Controlled-release preview. Opportunity → Bid Package.

Bid Foundry extracts likely requirements, dates, requested evidence, source references and human-review flags from procurement text supplied by the user. It can export CSV and JSON working records.

It does not submit bids, spend money, represent a company, publish externally, or replace qualified legal, engineering, procurement or licensing review. Human release is required.
