How it works

  1. JakeAI starts with the Dignity Check™: face, top of head, shirt collar.
  2. You tell it what you were doing the last time you remember having the glasses.
  3. It creates a prioritized search route and records every checked location in the Already Looked There Ledger™.
  4. If normal searching fails, Reconstruction Mode traces what you did after you stopped needing the glasses.
  5. When you find them, the session ends cleanly and—only with permission—the successful location can improve future searches.
